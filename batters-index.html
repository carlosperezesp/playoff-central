Positions come from each hitter's primary position, so a utility man appears at the spot he is listed for. Where a club has no regular at a spot, the outfield slots fall back to the next most-used outfielder and the DH to the most-used bat still unplaced. A backup is only offered where he has started at least 5 games with 60 plate appearances behind him and either takes 40% of the spot's starts or out-hits the man ahead of him by .080 of OPS — most spots have no such man, and those say nothing. Backups never count toward the lineup's OPS. Season totals are a hitter's full year, so a bat traded in July carries the numbers he put up elsewhere.
