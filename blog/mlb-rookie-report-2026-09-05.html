The Rookies Worth Watching in September

SEPTEMBER 5, 2026

The first-year players worth watching.

The rookie class doesn't have a runaway star, but it has depth in both dugout corners. A Tigers bat leads the hitters, a pair of arms sit under a 2.30 ERA, and a Royals kid is quietly climbing. Here's the freshman board as the pennant races heat up.

The rookie class

Top first-year players, hitters by OPS (minimum real playing time).

Eduardo Valencia paces the rookie hitters at a .924 OPS for Detroit, with Jonah Cox close behind at .883, but the power belongs to others: Esmerlyn Valdez has 16 homers for Pittsburgh and Munetaka Murakami leads the group with 29 for the White Sox. On the mound, Bradgley Rodriguez owns the shiniest line at a 1.99 ERA for San Diego, with Brandyn Garcia (2.20) and Didier Fuentes (2.27) right there.
