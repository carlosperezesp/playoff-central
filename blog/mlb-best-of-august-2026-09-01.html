August Belonged to the Bats in Kansas City and Chicago

SEPTEMBER 1, 2026

The hitters, sluggers and arms who owned August.

The book on August is closed, so here's who owned it. Jac Caglianone hit like the best player alive in Kansas City, Pete Crow-Armstrong did a little of everything for the Cubs, and Jesus Luzardo turned in a month starters dream about. A quick look back before September takes over.

August at the plate

August's best hitters, by OPS for the month.

Jac Caglianone led every hitter in August at a 1.113 OPS for the Royals, edging Pete Crow-Armstrong and Bryce Harper, who tied at 1.069. Crow-Armstrong's version came with real thump; Harper's was quieter power at five homers. Two Cubs made the top five, with Alex Bregman right there at 1.025.
