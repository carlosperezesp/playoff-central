The Week Reshuffles The Mound And The Bats

JUNE 19, 2026

Who climbed and who cooled over the past week.

This is a movers' edition, the day we track who climbed and who slipped over the past week. Dustin May had the loudest jump on the mound, while Bryce Harper led a quiet cooldown at the plate. Nobody fell off a cliff, but a few players changed color.
