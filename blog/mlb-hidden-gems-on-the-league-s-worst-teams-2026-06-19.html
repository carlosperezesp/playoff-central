Great Players, Bad Records, No Spotlight

JUNE 19, 2026

Good, healthy players having strong seasons on teams going nowhere.

Not every star gets to play in October. Today we pull the best healthy players off the ten worst teams in baseball, the ones doing real work for clubs going nowhere. Houston's Yordan Alvarez headlines a list that deserves better company.

Aces going to waste

The best arms (by form) on the five worst teams in each league — healthy, stuck on losing clubs. Tap a name for where they shine.

Cincinnati's Chase Burns is the find here, a 2.01 ERA and 1.02 WHIP with 95 strikeouts and eight wins on a losing club. The Angels send two: José Soriano keeps runs off the board at 2.79, while Reid Detmers' 100 strikeouts lead the group despite only three wins to show for it.
