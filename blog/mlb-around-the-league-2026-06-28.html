The Dodgers Hold, the East Wobbles

JUNE 28, 2026

A quick lap of the standings, offenses and pitching staffs.

Los Angeles still sits atop the power ranking with a plus-150 run differential and no challengers in sight. Behind them, the surprise is movement in the other direction: the Braves and Yankees both went 3-7 over their last ten and slid down the board.
