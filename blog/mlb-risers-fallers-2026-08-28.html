The White Sox Own the Week, Two Aces Cool

AUGUST 28, 2026

Who climbed and who cooled over the past week.

The scoreboard doesn't move much day to day, but the past week reshuffled a few things. Chicago's rookies caught fire, a couple of good arms went briefly cold, and the season's biggest bats mostly held serve. Here's who ran hot and who slipped.

Of the strongest arms, the ones who shed the most form this week.

A few sturdy arms lost a little shine: Chase Burns slipped five points to a form of 71, while Michael McGreevy (down five to 55) and Gavin Williams (down four to 59) also cooled. These are dips, not alarms.
