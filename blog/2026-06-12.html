Kurtz Climbs, Rice Cools, Marlins Surge

JUNE 12, 2026

The week belonged to bats on the move. Nick Kurtz added five home runs to charge up the leaderboard, while Ben Rice cooled off without losing his footing. And down in Miami, a 9-1 stretch dragged a .500 team into the conversation.

Turning it around

Kurtz led the home-run climbers with five new ones, lifting his OPS to .976; Yordan Alvarez (now 24, up three) and Shohei Ohtani (13, up three) kept pace. On the mound, Yoshinobu Yamamoto and Drew Rasmussen each gained five form points.
