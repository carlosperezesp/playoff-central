June Belonged to Pete Crow-Armstrong

JULY 1, 2026

The hitters, sluggers and arms who owned June.

June is in the books, and this one goes to Pete Crow-Armstrong, who hit like a man playing a different sport. The Cubs outfielder posted a 1.249 OPS with 11 home runs. On the mound, Logan Webb spent the month in a phone booth, allowing almost nothing.

June at the plate

June's best hitters, by OPS for the month.

Crow-Armstrong led all bats in June at 1.249, with Luis García Jr. close behind at 1.128 for Washington. Shohei Ohtani (1.091) and Junior Caminero (1.075) kept the top of the board loaded, and Dillon Dingler's 1.011 for Detroit was the month's quiet surprise.
