Toronto Crashes the Party, Milwaukee Holds

SEPTEMBER 6, 2026

A quick lap of the standings, offenses and pitching staffs.

The Brewers still sit on top despite a 3-4 week, but the real motion came further down. Toronto jumped eleven spots on a 5-1 week, and Houston climbed seven while still carrying a losing run differential. Order is starting to firm up as the races tighten.

Power Rankings

Power ranking — record, run differential and recent form, blended into one order. Tap a team for its week.

Milwaukee (88-55) keeps the top spot even after a quiet 3-4 week and a minus-8 run differential, with Cooper Pratt's 1.061 OPS the lone bright spot. The eye-catcher is Toronto, up eleven spots on a 5-1 week and a plus-24 run differential, powered by Kazuma Okamoto and a shutout start from Dylan Cease. Houston made noise too, climbing seven behind a 5-2 week even with a minus-37 season mark still to answer for.
