Kansas City Runs the Table, Storms the Board

AUGUST 24, 2026

Our team power ranking — record, run differential and recent form in one order.

Milwaukee is still on top, but the week belonged to the teams behind them. The Royals went 7-0 and leapt nineteen spots, while both the Dodgers and Phillies posted perfect weeks of their own. The top of the power ranking is getting crowded.

The Brewers hold the top spot on a 4-2 week and a season-best plus-161 run differential, with Garrett Mitchell going 8-for-12. The loudest climb is Kansas City, up nineteen into the top ten on a flawless 7-0 run behind Vinnie Pasquantino and a 10-for-27 week from Bobby Witt Jr. Boston jumped twelve to fourth after Mickey Gasper drove in nine over the week, and the Dodgers and Phillies each went 6-0 to sit second and third, Fernando Tatis Jr.'s 4-homer, 11-RBI week keeping San Diego steady at sixth.
