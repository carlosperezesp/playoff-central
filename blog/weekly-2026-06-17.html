Yankees Edge to the Top as Brewers Breathe Down Their Necks

JUNE 17, 2026

The Yankees took the top chair this week, but they earned it the hard way — 8-2 over their last ten, with Milwaukee a single game and four runs of differential behind them. The Dodgers own the best record and the fattest run margin at plus-143, yet sit third, which tells you how tight the air is at the summit. Down the standings, the wins are spreading out: the Cardinals, Nationals and Marlins all went 7-3, the kind of week that turns June from a slog into a race.
