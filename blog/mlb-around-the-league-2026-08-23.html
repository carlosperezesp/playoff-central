Philadelphia Sweeps a Week, Climbs the Board

AUGUST 23, 2026

A quick lap of the standings, offenses and pitching staffs.

The Brewers still sit on top, but the loud story is Philadelphia. The Phillies went 6-0 this week and jumped ten spots in the power ranking. Milwaukee and the Dodgers held the top two, both at 5-1.

Power Rankings

Power ranking — record, run differential and recent form, blended into one order. Tap a team for its week.

Milwaukee keeps the top spot after a 5-1 week and a plus-31 run differential, with Garrett Mitchell going 8-for-11. The real mover is Philadelphia, up ten to third on a perfect 6-0 run behind Alec Bohm's 11 RBI and a sharp Aaron Nola start. Cleveland made the biggest quiet leap, up fifteen into the top ten on a 4-2 week and Jo Adell's 10 RBI.
