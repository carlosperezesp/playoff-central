Alonso's Four-Homer Week, and a Few Cold Spells

SEPTEMBER 25, 2026

Who climbed and who cooled over the past week.

The week's climbers hit, and the fallers mostly went quiet at the plate. Pete Alonso homered four times to reach 40, while a couple of good bats slipped a few points. Nobody's arm lost ground, which is what late September looks like.

Hot at the plate

The week's hottest hitters — top OPS over the last 7 days (min at-bats), with their game line.

Angel Martínez had the loudest bat of the week, 8-for-17 with three homers for a 1.589 OPS, just ahead of Zack Gelof's four-homer, eight-RBI stretch in Oakland. Mookie Betts and Will Smith both scorched it for the Dodgers.
