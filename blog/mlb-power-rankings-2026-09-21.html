Brewers Hold, but Dodgers Made the Loudest Week

SEPTEMBER 21, 2026

Our team power ranking — record, run differential and recent form in one order.

Milwaukee still sits atop our power ranking at 98-58, and a 5-1 week keeps them there. But the Dodgers went 6-1 and outscored people by plus-23 doing it, so the gap at the top is thinner than the standings suggest.

The Brewers earned first with steady work: a 5-1 week, Cooper Pratt going 6-for-15 with four steals and Robert Gasser throwing seven scoreless. Right behind, Los Angeles rode Kyle Tucker's three homers and a 1.577 OPS to a plus-23 week, and the Padres kept climbing, up two spots to fourth on the back of Jake Cronenworth's 13-for-28. Cleveland is the surprise, jumping four to eighth after a 5-1 run got the Guardians back over .500 at 81-75.
