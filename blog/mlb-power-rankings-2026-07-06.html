The Mariners Surge While the Top Three Hold

JULY 6, 2026

Our team power ranking — record, run differential and recent form in one order.

The Dodgers, Brewers and Rays still fill the top three spots in our power ranking, all steady from a week ago. The biggest jump belongs to Seattle, which vaulted seven spots after a 5-1 week. Here's how the league stacks up right now.

Los Angeles stays at the top at 59-32, going 5-2 behind Miguel Rojas (7-for-13) and seven shutout innings from Yoshinobu Yamamoto, with Milwaukee and Tampa Bay close behind. Seattle is the mover, up seven to tenth on a 5-1 week and a plus-23 run differential, capped by Logan Gilbert's scoreless outing. Boston and Chicago's White Sox each climbed three, while the Cubs slipped a spot despite Dansby Swanson's five-homer, eleven-RBI week.
