The Braves Take the Wheel, No One Runs Away

SEPTEMBER 7, 2026

Our team power ranking — record, run differential and recent form in one order.

For the first time in a while there's a new club at the top, and it isn't the one with the best record. Atlanta grabbed the number-one spot even at 85-58, while Milwaukee's 88-56 mark slid to third after a rough week. Nobody's separating themselves; the top of the board is bunched.

Power Rankings

Power ranking — record, run differential and recent form, blended into one order. Tap a team for its week.

The Braves led things behind Ronald Acuna Jr.'s 11-for-25 week with three homers and a 1.303 OPS, enough to nudge past the Dodgers, who went 4-2 on Mookie Betts' 11 RBI and a shutout six from Blake Snell. Milwaukee's slip to third is honest math — a 3-4 week and a minus-9 run differential — while the real climb belongs to Toronto, up seven spots on a 4-2 week and a plus-12 mark, with Dylan Cease spinning six scoreless. Pittsburgh quietly rose five too, riding Jared Jones and his nine strikeouts over six clean innings.
