The Bats Speak Loudest, in Houston and the Bronx

JUNE 10, 2026

Pitching wins the ledgers, but on this day the offense holds the floor. Yordan Alvarez is carrying an OPS north of 1.066 and the kind of pace that makes the record book nervous; Ben Rice has answered in the Bronx, just over 1.000 himself even as last week trimmed a little off the top. The Dodgers and Yankees lead the league in slugging the way they lead it in everything else, and the long ball has found some new tenants. Today we read the season through the men holding the lumber.

Best offenses

The Dodgers sit atop the OPS table at .789, the only team to clear that mark, with the Yankees at .763 and the Braves close behind. Notice the Nationals, fourth at .742, keeping company they're not supposed to keep.
