Rays Grab the Top While Padres Charge

AUGUST 16, 2026

A quick lap of the standings, offenses and pitching staffs.

It's a tight race at the top, and Tampa Bay now sits first at 74-48 after a 4-2 week. Below them the Padres are the story, surging five spots on the strength of a 5-1 run.

And keep an eye on San Diego, up to seventh after going 5-1.
