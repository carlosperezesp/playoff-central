The Ballots Are Open, and the Names Surprise

JUNE 11, 2026

Mid-June is when the award conversation stops being polite speculation and starts leaving fingerprints on the standings. The names doing the leading aren't all the ones we penciled in March: a rookie from Japan is mashing on the South Side, a flamethrower in Milwaukee is making hitters look foolish, and Yordan Alvarez is quietly bending the season to his will. Here is where the three races stand, with the numbers doing the talking.

MVP watch

Yordan Alvarez tops the hitting board at a 1.066 OPS with 22 homers, on a pace history takes seriously, while Ben Rice and his 1.006 mark give the Yankees a second voice in the room. Shohei Ohtani lurks at .964, as he tends to.

Rookie of the Year watch

Munetaka Murakami has turned the rookie race into a formality for now — a .938 OPS and 20 home runs for the White Sox, numbers that belong on the elite-hitter list, not the freshman one. Bryce Eldridge (.906) and Sal Stewart (13 homers) follow at a respectful distance; on the mound, San Diego's Bradgley Rodriguez owns a tidy 2.01 ERA.
