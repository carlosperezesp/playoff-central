Boston Crashes the Party at Number Three

JULY 13, 2026

Our team power ranking — record, run differential and recent form in one order.

The top two didn't budge, but a team ten games under .500 just muscled its way into third. Boston won all six games it played this week, and that kind of run is hard to argue with, even from a losing record.
