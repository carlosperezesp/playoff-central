The Bats Report: A New Home Run King Hits 40

SEPTEMBER 2, 2026

The league's hottest offenses and hitters, by OPS and home runs.

Kyle Schwarber is the first hitter to 40 home runs, and the Phillies have the leader. Yordan Alvarez still owns the best overall bat, but Pete Crow-Armstrong put on the loudest show of the week. Here's where the hitting stands as September opens.

Team OPS — on-base plus slugging. Higher is better.

The Cubs still set the pace with a .773 team OPS, a bit clear of the Nationals at .757 and the Dodgers at .756. Milwaukee and Colorado round out the top five, both at .738.
