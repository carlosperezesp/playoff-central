The Dodgers Climb, The Capital Stirs

JUNE 15, 2026

The standings settled into a familiar hierarchy this week, then quietly rearranged the furniture beneath it. Los Angeles, with the game's loudest run differential at plus-142, moved up to claim the top spot, while the Yankees rode a 7-3 stretch to second. But the real motion came from the bottom of the top ten: Washington and Miami each surged seven places, both 7-3 over their last ten, the sort of June heat that turns a wild-card hopeful into a wild-card believer. The Braves, meanwhile, slid three spots despite the same 46 wins as the Dodgers — proof that this list rewards who's moving, not who arrived early.
