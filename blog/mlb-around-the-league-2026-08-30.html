Atlanta Sweeps Its Way Into the Top Three

AUGUST 30, 2026

A quick lap of the standings, offenses and pitching staffs.

The Braves went 6-0 this week and jumped six spots to third in the power ranking. Nobody else near the top moved much, but Atlanta didn't leave a choice.
