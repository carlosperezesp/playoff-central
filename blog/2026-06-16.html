The Yankees Quietly Stack Up Zeroes

JUNE 16, 2026

Pitching wins arguments, and right now the loudest case belongs to the Bronx, where New York has climbed three spots to the top of the rankings on the back of the league's stingiest staff. The Yankees lead the majors in team ERA and ride an 8-2 streak that's mostly about run prevention. But the single most arresting line in the game belongs to a 24-year-old in Milwaukee carrying a 0.74 WHIP, and behind him a handful of arms are finding their best form just as June turns serious.

Best staffs on the mound

Five staffs sit under a 3.65 ERA, and the Yankees (3.30) edge the Dodgers and Braves at the front. The separation is razor-thin, but Los Angeles keeps the cleanest sheet of all — a 1.08 WHIP that says traffic almost never reaches base.
