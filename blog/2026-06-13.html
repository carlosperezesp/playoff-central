The Newcomers Make Their Case In June

JUNE 13, 2026

Every June the game refreshes itself, and this one is no exception. Munetaka Murakami arrived in Chicago with a reputation and has lived up to it, his 20 home runs already louder than most veterans can manage. Behind him a quieter class is doing its work: Bryce Eldridge swinging a heavy bat in San Francisco, a pair of young arms in San Diego and Anaheim keeping their ERAs under control. The names are unfamiliar today; check back in October.

The rookie class

Murakami's .938 OPS leads the freshman hitters, but Bryce Eldridge's .956 mark in San Francisco edges it on rate, and Colt Emerson is making early noise in Seattle. On the mound, Bradgley Rodriguez (2.23) and Walbert Ureña (2.44) are doing more than holding their own.
